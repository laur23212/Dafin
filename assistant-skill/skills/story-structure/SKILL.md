# Dafin Story Structure Director

- Write a compact spine: premise → orientation → change/obstacle → escalation → payoff/response. Not every project needs this structure; preserve authentic chronology in nonfiction.
- Edit a scene when the dramatic question or intention changes. Protect the viewer's understanding of who wants what.
- For performance-led scenes, preserve eye-lines, body language, geography, sound bridges and silence.
- J/L cuts serve continuity only when the actual sound/image handles exist. Don't invent source extensions.
- Protect truth in interviews and documentaries: do not manufacture cause/effect, splice quotes misleadingly or insert unlabeled reenactments.
- If the user selects Cinematic, do not inject kinetic YouTube captions by default, even on a 'punchy' energy setting.
- Evaluate by emotion, clarity, rhythm and economy; the absence of an effect can be the correct design choice.

## YouTube-first research overlay
For story-driven YouTube, inspect viewer promise, character goal, repeatable obstacles and paid-off story threads before applying movie-like pacing. Hillier-Smith's emotional editing advice and Paddy Galloway's Mark Rober/Airrack analyses motivate the inspection; they are not compulsory templates. Use `dafin_story_audit` only with actual editorial annotations.

## Nested storytelling coordination
For YouTube long-form, defer all macro/micro arc analysis and optional narrative rewards to `youtube-story-strategist` and `dafin_audit_narrative_rewards`. This Skill remains responsible for truthful continuity and emotion, not invented dopamine triggers or mandatory beats. Protect genuine emotional holds and preserve documentary chronology.

## v0.8: cinema narrative caution
Film scenes are not obligated to carry YouTube-style hooks or a mini-payoff every few seconds. Build questions, perspective and change through genuine action, performance and sound. A subjective or nonlinear chronology requires explicit intent; preserve documentary facts and actual emotional continuity. Hollywood three-act structure is an option, not a validator. Use `editorial-director` for cut grammar; do not duplicate its technical decisions.
