# Editorial decision rules (original guidance)

Apply these as context-dependent questions, not a stopwatch. If footage has not been inspected, recommendations must be conditional.

## The decision ladder
1. **Truth and meaning:** Preserve what happened, what was said, and the causal order. No misleading cutaway/eyeline/reaction.
2. **Emotional intention:** Does the viewer need to laugh, understand, anticipate, worry, or breathe?
3. **Cognitive load:** Do not add an independent graphic while the viewer reads important text or follows a complex action.
4. **Pace and contrast:** Use variation. A slow shot after a fast sequence can increase impact; nonstop speed becomes flat.
5. **Movement and continuity:** Watch the cut on actual playback. Direction, motion, and eye trace may override symmetric shot durations.

## YouTube creator (energetic sections)
- Make a *new meaningful event* roughly every few seconds when the material supports it: a claim, example, reaction, demonstration, punchline, shot-change, or graphic.
- A visual change is not necessarily a cut. Do not add an effect if a great expression or a necessary demo is already holding attention.
- In a typical 2-second on-screen title, spend the available time on reading, not decorative entrance/exit animation. If the copy needs longer, shorten it or extend the clip.
- Use punch-ins for emotional or rhetorical emphasis, not as a repeated predictable tick.
- Remove dead air that conveys nothing, but keep pauses that reveal emotion, anticipation, or an unspoken response.

## Cinematic / film
- Prioritize performance, spatial orientation, mood and sound bridges.
- Avoid over-explaining atmosphere with bouncing text, whooshes or permanent camera movement.
- Cut when the audience has absorbed the emotional beat, not because a target shot duration has expired.
- Protect continuity of action unless an expressive break is deliberately requested.

## Explain vs demonstrate
- **Explain:** use diagrams or labelled inserts when they reduce verbal complexity.
- **Demonstrate:** maintain uninterrupted steps and permit longer holds to verify a real UI or physical action.
- **Prove:** B-roll must genuinely establish the claim; an unrelated stock clip is decoration, not evidence.

## Before any edit
Request real media and timing metadata. Produce a plan that names why each change is needed; do not pretend to have found a pause or B-roll asset without looking.
