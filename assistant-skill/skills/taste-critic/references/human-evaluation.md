# Human preference baseline before claiming better taste

Use legal sample footage. Lock brief, Dafin build, assets, model, token budget, video runtime and output resolution. Generate a baseline edit without Skills and a Skill-assisted edit; randomize A/B presentation without naming the model. Include a tie option. Ask independently about comprehension, rhythm, text legibility, sound and emotional outcome. Preserve disagreement and the reasons given; do not turn one editor's preferences into universal rules. Keep signed project revision and inspected native media receipts separately from subjective votes. Do not learn user taste across projects without explicit control and consent.

Production gate: no claims of superior videos or improved retention from unit tests. Primary official diagnostic context: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en .
