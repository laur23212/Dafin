# Honest, minimal visual review (original guidance)

1. Confirm creative brief, selected axes, source project ID and project revision.
2. Ask Dafin for authenticated frame captures or a short rendered sequence around a suspected issue. A single frame can show clipping or hierarchy, **not** easing quality or cut rhythm.
3. Request audio clips only when listening/measurement is relevant. A metadata peak warning cannot establish true-peak clipping or unintelligible dialogue.
4. For each critique, write: **observed**, **why it matters in this style**, **specific minimal correction**, **confidence**, **evidence IDs**, **what remains unverified**.
5. Link references to the same revision and the requested timestamp. An arbitrary evidence handle supplied by a caller is not authentication.
6. For subjective comparison, generate at most two defensible alternatives; give pros/cons and ask user to choose if the preference is uncertain.
7. Prefer local correction to full re-edit. Keep a path to Undo and preserve deliberate holds, documentary truth and accessibility.
8. Stop after one focused correction/review pass unless asked otherwise. Don't invent a 'beauty score' or audience retention prediction.

## Critique examples
- Weak: "Looks amateurish."
- Better, **after inspection**: "At 00:12.4 the 8-word headline covers the speaker's eyes for the entire reaction (frame evidence X). Move it to lower-left and shorten to a 3-word claim; check captions remain safe at 9:16 crop."
- If not inspected: "The plan contains a potential text-face collision at 00:12.4; please capture that frame before I judge the composition."
