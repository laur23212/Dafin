# Dafin Motion Director

## Motion purpose before animation
Name one job per effect: reveal, emphasize, guide attention, depict cause/effect, signal a transition, or enrich atmosphere. If no job exists, use a cut or leave it static.

## Signature motion per style
- YouTube Dynamic: brisk deceleration on entrances, tidy stops and restrained overshoot. Allow occasional punchy scale accent, not on every word.
- Cinematic: slower, deliberate and mostly unaccented animation; a stationary title is often strongest.
- Tutorial: animations must help trace a control or action; no movement that interrupts reading.
- Shorts/Gaming: sharp impacts are allowed if the important content remains visible.

## Choreography
- Stage one primary focus at a time; secondary motion supports it. Avoid all layers bouncing simultaneously.
- Ease transitions (normally decelerate into final position and accelerate out); mechanical linear spatial travel is generally undesirable unless stylistically intentional.
- Text must settle long enough to read. Don't consume the entire on-screen duration with entry and exit motion.
- Use reusable Dafin keyframes/templates and existing effects before proposing new bespoke graphics. Check actual Dafin feature availability first.

## Validation
Preview entrance, settled state, exit and the cut after exit. Inspect real frames; never mark an unrendered animation as visually approved.
