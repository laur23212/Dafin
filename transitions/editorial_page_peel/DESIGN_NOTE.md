# Editorial Page Peel

Category: editorial

What viewer sees: An outgoing shot peels away from a diagonal fold, revealing the next scene under a shaded sheet curl and fine fold highlight; a tactile editorial transition without extra textures.

Controls: Paper fold width, Fold direction, Fold shadow, Paper underside shade.

Caveat: Best for tutorials, recipe steps, travel chapters and photo montages. Intentional stylized paper, not physically accurate 3D simulation.

All code authored originally for Dafin. No embedded imagery, fonts, logos or commercial assets.
